# Уникальные изображения разделов

Созданы встроенным Imagegen. Каждый сюжет используется ровно в одном месте страницы. PNG сохранены рядом, адаптивные варианты — в `src/assets/editorial/<имя>/`.

## hero-residence

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: unique thematic photo for a private realtor website. Primary request: A distinctive sunlit contemporary detached ivory townhouse entrance, tall warm timber front door, pale limestone steps, an olive tree in a large clay pot and understated sage planting. Vertical-friendly composition inside a landscape photograph, entrance occupies center and right. No apartment balconies. Color palette: warm ivory, muted sage green, soft olive and natural oak; refined understated editorial photography, natural materials, realistic soft daylight. Landscape 3:2. This scene must be visually distinct from apartment facade, mortgage calculator and glass shield imagery already used elsewhere. No people, no words, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no prices. This is decorative thematic imagery, not a property listing.
